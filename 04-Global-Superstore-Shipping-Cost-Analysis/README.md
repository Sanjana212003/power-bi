## Overview

This Power BI report analyzes **shipping costs across different product categories** using the Global Superstore dataset. The dashboard focuses on understanding category-wise shipping expenses, comparing shipping costs across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies, and identifying differences in shipping cost contribution.

The report uses multiple visualizations to provide both numerical and comparative insights into shipping cost performance.

## Key Analysis

* Shipping Cost by Category
* Category-wise shipping cost distribution
* Comparison of Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies
* Shipping cost contribution by category
* Comparative analysis of category-level shipping expenses
* Identification of the category with the highest shipping cost

## Dashboard Highlights

The report provides a visual analysis of shipping costs through:

* **Horizontal Bar Chart** – Compares total shipping costs across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
* **Donut Chart** – Shows the percentage contribution of each category to the overall shipping cost.
* **Combined Comparison Chart** – Provides a comparative view of shipping costs across product categories.
* **Business Insight** – Highlights the category with the highest shipping cost and compares it with the lowest-cost category.

## Key Business Insights

* **Technology** has the highest shipping cost at approximately **507.05K**.
* **Furniture** has a shipping cost of approximately **440.32K**.
* **Office Supplies** has the lowest shipping cost at approximately **405.45K**.
* Technology contributes approximately **37.48%** of the category-level shipping cost.
* Furniture contributes approximately **32.55%**.
* Office Supplies contributes approximately **29.97%**.
* The dashboard indicates that Technology's shipping cost is approximately **25.06% higher than Office Supplies**.

These comparisons help identify categories with relatively higher shipping expenses and provide a basis for further analysis of logistics and operational costs.
