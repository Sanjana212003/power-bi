## Overview

This Power BI report analyzes sales and profit performance using the **Global Superstore dataset**. The dashboard provides insights into product-level profitability, country-wise sales distribution, and overall business performance through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

## Key Analysis

* Profit by Product Name
* Country-wise Sales Distribution
* Identification of high-profit products
* Geographical analysis of sales
* Overall Quantity, Sales, and Profit performance
* Comparison of product-level profitability

## Dashboard Highlights

The report provides a visual overview of business performance through:

* **Horizontal Bar Chart** – Compares products based on their total profit and highlights high-profit products.
* **Geographical Map** – Displays the distribution of sales across different countries and regions.
* **KPI Cards** – Summarizes the overall quantity, sales, and profit generated.
* **Product Performance Analysis** – Helps identify products contributing significantly to overall profitability.
