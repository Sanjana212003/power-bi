## Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of sales performance using the **Global Superstore dataset**. The dashboard focuses on category-wise sales, regional performance, product-level analysis, order-date trends, and overall sales performance through interactive visualizations, filters, and KPI reporting.

## Key Analysis

* Sales by Category
* Sales by Region
* Sales distribution across different regions and countries
* Product-level sales analysis
* Category and product hierarchy analysis
* Order date and sales relationship
* Year-wise and region-wise filtering
* Overall sales performance

## Dashboard Highlights

The report provides a visual overview of sales performance through:

* **Category-wise Sales Comparison** – Compares total sales across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
* **Sales and Order Analysis** – Visualizes the relationship between sales values and the count of order dates.
* **Regional Sales Analysis** – Shows the contribution of different regions to total sales using a pie chart.
* **Product Hierarchy** – Provides a detailed category and product-level view for exploring individual products.
* **Interactive Filters** – Allows users to filter the report by year and region.
* **Total Sales KPI** – Displays the overall sales value of **12.64M**.

## Category-wise Sales

The dashboard compares sales across the three major product categories:

* **Technology** – Highest sales among the three categories.
* **Furniture** – Second-highest sales category.
* **Office Supplies** – Third-highest sales category.

This comparison helps identify which product categories contribute most significantly to overall sales.
