# Global Superstore Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Overview

This is a **multi-page Power BI Business Intelligence report** developed using the **Global Superstore dataset**. The dashboard provides an interactive analysis of business performance across sales, profit, quantity, products, categories, states, countries, and regions.

The report is designed to provide both a high-level executive overview and detailed insights into geographical and product-level sales performance.

## Key Features

* **Multi-page Power BI Report** – Organized into Executive Summary, Sales, Regional Performance, and Products pages.
* **Interactive Filters** – Year and Region filters allow users to dynamically explore the data.
* **KPI Cards** – Displays Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Quantity.
* **Geographical Visualization** – Maps sales distribution across countries and regions.
* **Category Analysis** – Compares sales performance across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
* **Product Hierarchy** – Enables users to explore categories and individual products.
* **State-level Analysis** – Compares sales performance across different states.
* **Data Visualization** – Uses charts, maps, tables, and KPI visualizations to communicate business insights.

## Business Insights

The dashboard provides several useful business insights:

* **Technology** is the highest-performing sales category, contributing approximately **37.53%** of total sales.
* **Furniture** contributes approximately **32.51%** of total sales.
* **Office Supplies** contributes approximately **29.96%** of total sales.
* The business generated approximately **12.64M in total sales** and **1.47M in total profit**.
* The geographical visualizations help identify differences in sales performance across countries, states, and regions.
* Interactive year and region filters allow users to examine business performance for specific periods and geographical areas.

## Conclusion

The **Global Superstore Business Intelligence Dashboard** provides a comprehensive and interactive view of business performance. By combining executive-level KPIs with detailed sales, geographical, regional, and product analysis, the report helps users understand sales patterns and identify high-performing categories and locations.

This project demonstrates practical skills in **Power BI, Business Intelligence, data visualization, interactive dashboard development, KPI reporting, geographical analysis, and data storytelling**.
