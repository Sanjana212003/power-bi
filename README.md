# 📊 Global Superstore Power BI Analytics

A collection of **Power BI Business Intelligence dashboards and data analysis reports** developed using the **Global Superstore dataset**.

This repository demonstrates practical skills in **data visualization, business intelligence, KPI reporting, sales analysis, profitability analysis, geographical analysis, product analysis, and interactive dashboard development**.

The dashboards transform raw Global Superstore data into interactive visual reports that help identify business trends, category performance, regional patterns, product profitability, and shipping-cost behavior.

---

## Repository Overview

This repository contains multiple Power BI reports focused on different aspects of the Global Superstore business data.

### Dashboards Included

| Dashboard                                             | Primary Focus                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Global Superstore Profit and Sales Analysis**       | Product profitability, geographical sales, and business KPIs |
| **Global Superstore Sales Dashboard**                 | Category, product, region, and sales analysis                |
| **Global Superstore Business Intelligence Dashboard** | Multi-page executive, sales, regional, and product analysis  |
| **Global Superstore Shipping Cost Analysis**          | Category-wise shipping cost and logistics analysis           |

# 4. Global Superstore Shipping Cost Analysis

### Focus

This dashboard analyzes **shipping costs across the major product categories**.

### Key Analysis

* Shipping Cost by Category
* Category-wise shipping cost distribution
* Comparison of shipping costs
* Technology vs Furniture vs Office Supplies
* Category contribution to shipping costs
* Identification of high shipping-cost categories

### Shipping Cost Analysis

| Category            | Shipping Cost | Contribution |
| ------------------- | ------------: | -----------: |
| **Technology**      |   **507.05K** |   **37.48%** |
| **Furniture**       |   **440.32K** |   **32.55%** |
| **Office Supplies** |   **405.45K** |   **29.97%** |

### Key Insight

**Technology** has the highest shipping cost among the three major categories, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.

The dashboard indicates that Technology's shipping cost is approximately **25.06% higher than Office Supplies**.
